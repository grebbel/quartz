# Cyberbiosecurity bibliography

This bibliography contains the four literature sources currently available from the `CyberBioSecurity` collection. The source PDFs remain in `01_SOURCE`; this page publishes citation metadata and short relevance notes for the wiki.

## Foundations and definitions

### Murch et al. (2018)

Murch, Randall S., William K. So, Wallace G. Buchholz, Sanjay Raman, and Jean Peccoud. 2018. "[Cyberbiosecurity: An Emerging New Discipline to Help Safeguard the Bioeconomy](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbioe.2018.00039)." _Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology_ 6:39.

This article frames cyberbiosecurity as an emerging discipline concerned with threats at the interfaces between biological, cyber, cyber-physical, supply-chain, and infrastructure systems. It provides a foundation for defining the scope of the wiki and the proposed assessment tool.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/fbioe-06-00039.pdf`

## Current challenges and technology

### Al-Eitan, Jaouni, and Mihyar (2025)

Al-Eitan, Laith, Haytham Jaouni, and Ahmad Mihyar. 2025. "Addressing Cyberbiosecurity Challenges in the Modern Era of Biotechnology and Artificial Intelligence." Jordan University of Science and Technology.

This review discusses cyberbiosecurity threats to biological data, systems, and infrastructure as biotechnology and artificial intelligence become increasingly digitized. It is relevant to the wiki's treatment of AI-integrated processes, data protection, and connected laboratory systems.

No DOI was stated in the available source PDF.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/PDF+Galley_LaithAlEitan+et+al.pdf`

### Anjum et al. (2025)

Anjum, Saima, et al. 2025. "[Cyber-Biosecurity Challenges in Next-Generation Sequencing: A Comprehensive Analysis of Emerging Threat Vectors](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3552069)." _IEEE Access_.

This article addresses cyberbiosecurity risks associated with next-generation sequencing, including the security implications of increasingly connected sequencing workflows and biological data systems.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/Anjum et al. - 2025 - Cyber-Biosecurity Challenges in Next-Generation Sequencing A Comprehensive Analysis of Emerging Thr.pdf`

## International peace and security

### Fouad (2024)

Fouad, Noran Shafik. 2024. "[Cyberbiosecurity in the New Normal: Cyberbio Risks, Pre-Emptive Security, and the Global Governance of Bioinformation](https://doi.org/10.1017/eis.2024.19)." _European Journal of International Security_.

This article contributes a governance and anticipatory-security perspective on cyberbiosecurity risks and the international governance of bioinformation, providing context for the project's policy and responsible-use dimensions.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/Fouad - 2024 - Cyberbiosecurity in the new normal Cyberbio risks, pre-emptive security, and the global governance.pdf`

### Mazeaud and Kastelic (2025)

Mazeaud, Louison, and Andraz Kastelic. 2025. "[Cyberbiosecurity: A Matter of International Peace and Security?](https://doi.org/10.37559/WMD/25/CBW/02)." United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research.

This report considers cyberbiosecurity risks created by the convergence of information and biological technologies in relation to international peace, security, and the prevention of biological misuse. It supports the wiki's international, policy, and dual-use perspectives.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/UNIDIR_Cyberbiosecurity_matter_international_peace_security.pdf`

## Pandemic preparedness and biological information

### Mueller (2021)

Mueller, Siguna. 2021. "[Facing the 2020 Pandemic: What Does Cyberbiosecurity Want Us to Know to Safeguard the Future?](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bsheal.2020.09.007)." _Biosafety and Health_.

This article uses the COVID-19 pandemic as a starting point for considering cyberbiosecurity lessons and future preparedness across biological and digital systems.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/Mueller - 2021 - Facing the 2020 pandemic What does cyberbiosecurity want us to know to safeguard the future.pdf`

## Biosteganography and dual-use questions

### Brunet (2016)

Brunet, T. D. P. 2016. "[Aims and Methods of Biosteganography](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiotec.2016.03.044)." _Journal of Biotechnology_.

This paper is relevant to the wiki's emerging-topic review because it concerns the concealment of information in biological systems. It should be treated as a source for governance and risk questions, not as operational guidance.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/Brunet - 2016 - Aims and methods of biosteganography.pdf`

### Imperiale and Casadevall (2026)

Imperiale, Michael J., and Arturo Casadevall. 2026. "[Mirror Microbial Life and the Dual-Use Dilemma](https://doi.org/10.1128/msphere.00342-26)." _mSphere_.

This article addresses the dual-use implications of mirror microbial life. The topic is recorded as an open research question for the scope and governance pages; the publication date should be verified against the source record if timing is material.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/Mirror microbial life and the dual use dilemma.pdf`

## Critical and societal perspectives

### Fouad (2026)

Fouad, Noran Shafik. 2026. "[Constructing the Future in and of Cyberbiosecurity: Cyber-Bio Convergence and (In)Security Imaginaries](https://doi.org/10.1093/isagsq/ksag101)." _International Studies Quarterly_.

This article examines how digitization and cyber-bio convergence shape perceptions of biological risk, securitization, militarization, and preventive security policy. It adds a critical perspective for interpreting governance, responsible use, and the societal consequences of cyberbiosecurity.

Source file: `01_SOURCE/ksag101.pdf`
