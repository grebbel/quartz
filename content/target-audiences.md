# Target audiences

The initial guidance is intended for organizations and professionals who manage, secure, regulate, or support biological work.

## Primary audiences

- Laboratory management
- Biosafety and biosecurity officers
- Research facility managers
- Information security and IT teams supporting laboratories
- Public health and biotechnology organizations
- Bioinformatics, pharmaceutical, biomedical, and biomanufacturing organizations
- Biological data, cloud, and outsourced-service providers
- Policymakers and international-governance stakeholders

## Geographic focus

The project includes a focus on nations in East and Central Africa. Guidance should be practical for different levels of digital maturity, infrastructure, staffing, funding, and regulatory capacity.

## Audience needs to validate

- A shared vocabulary for cyberbiosecurity risks
- Practical controls and responsibilities
- A way to assess current maturity and priorities
- Clear escalation and incident-response pathways
- Examples that do not assume high-cost infrastructure
- Guidance that connects technical, biological, organizational, and policy concerns
